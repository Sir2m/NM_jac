Training Procedure
Models were trained and tested in kaggle environment
on T4 x2 TPU. We used "Braindecode" module models.
Training parameters are provided in the results seciton
and the notebooks link are provided in teh repo link. The
models are trained for 800 epochs (excpct EEGConformer)
with an Adam optimizer at a learning rate of 0.001 and a
batch size of 64. The used hyperparameters were chosen
based on the models' papers. For our model, NH_PINN,
hyperparameters were chosen based on random experimetns.
EEGConformer, since it is an attention based model, had
only 100 epochs. 

Rsults

We evaluated our model through cross validation method
on BCI comptetion IV 2a dataset. We trained 5 Models on
each subject and we selected the maximum test accuracy out of
them then we used the average of these selected results. We
compared our  model to 3 main models:
EEGNet: as it is the emerging EEG CNN model, and based
on reviews, it is becoming the standard CNN model  in
EEG [Cite the review]
TCNet: As a frontire model that showed a good results 
EEGConformer: As an heavy weight attention based model.
